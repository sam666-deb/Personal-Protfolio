Hi, I'm Ahsan — welcome to the first post on my new blog.

I've spent the last few years moving between two worlds: **Unity/AR/VR development** (my background from Daffodil International University, where I also trained students in game development) and **full-stack web and mobile engineering**, which has become a bigger part of my work while completing my Master of Information Technology and Systems at the University of Tasmania.

This blog is where I'll write about the space in between — things like:

- Building real-time full-stack apps (like [CommuteCast](https://commute-cast-six.vercel.app), a live transit + weather companion)
- Shipping cross-platform mobile apps with React Native and Expo
- Unity, AR/VR, and immersive interaction design
- Notes from coursework and research as I explore AI/ML and HCI more deeply
- Practical write-ups on tools, bugs, and decisions — the kind of post I wish I'd found when I was stuck

## Why now?

A portfolio is a snapshot. A blog is a log. I wanted a place to think out loud about what I'm building and learning, not just show the finished result — partly for future employers and collaborators, and partly for future-me, who will definitely forget how I solved these problems the first time.

If you're a fellow student, developer, or researcher and something here is useful (or wrong!), reach out using the button below — I'd like to hear from you.

More soon.
